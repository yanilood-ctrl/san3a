## 1. Introduction

San3a is a mobile application that connects people who need practical work done with the tradespeople and contracting companies who can do it.

As a **customer**, you can find a provider, send them a service request, follow that request until the work is finished, message the provider along the way, and leave a rating afterwards.

As a **professional** or a **contractor**, you receive those requests, accept the ones you want, keep the customer updated as the work progresses, and build a public rating from completed jobs.

An **admin** keeps the marketplace healthy — managing accounts and orders, responding to complaints, moderating reviews and conversations, and sending announcements.

The interface is in English. Individual pieces of text — chat messages, provider descriptions, service details, reviews, and help articles — can be translated on demand into Arabic, Hebrew, or English without changing the rest of the app.

## 2. Getting Started

### Opening the App

Launch San3a on your Android device. If you have signed in before, the app opens directly on the home screen for your account type. If not, you arrive at the sign-in screen.

### Creating an Account

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Create Account**.
2. Choose your account type. Three options are available:
   - **Customer** — you want to hire someone
   - **Professional** — you are an individual tradesperson
   - **Contractor** — you represent a company with workers
3. Enter your full name, email address, phone number, and a password.
4. Fill in the fields that appear for your account type. Customers are asked for their city and address details; professionals and contractors are additionally asked for information such as work area, specialty, and experience.
5. Tap the sign-up button to finish.

Your account type is set at registration and determines everything you see in the app. The **Admin** role cannot be created here — it is assigned to existing accounts.

### Logging In

1. Open the app and enter your email address and password.
2. Tap **Sign In**.
3. You are taken to the home screen for your account type.

If your account has been suspended or removed by an administrator, you will be shown a notice instead of the normal home screen.

### Logging Out

- **Customer:** open the **Profile** tab and use the logout option.
- **Professional:** open the **Profile** tab and tap the logout button in the top-right of the header.
- **Contractor:** open the **Profile** tab and use the logout option.
- **Admin:** open the **Profile** tab and tap the logout button in the top-right of the header.

Logging out returns you to the sign-in screen and clears the navigation history, so the back button will not return you into the app.

## 3. Customer Guide

The customer app has four tabs along the bottom: **Home**, **Messages**, **Orders**, and **Profile**.

### Customer Home

The Home tab is your starting point. From here you can:

- Search using the search field at the top
- Browse service categories, or open the full list of categories
- See providers and browse their profiles
- Open the AI Service Assistant
- Open your notifications and favourites

### Finding a Professional

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Either tap a category tile, or open the full category list to see everything available.
3. Select the category that matches the work you need.
4. Browse the providers listed under that category.
5. Tap a provider to open their profile.

You can also type into the search field on Home to look for a service or a provider directly.

### Viewing a Professional Profile

A provider profile shows their name and photograph, their rating, the services they offer with prices, their work area and availability, and the reviews other customers have left.

From this screen you can:

- Add the provider to your **Favorites**
- Start a conversation with them
- Create a service request
- Read reviews, and translate a review or the provider's description if it is not in a language you read
- Report the provider if something is wrong

### Creating a Service Request / Order

1. Open the provider's profile.
2. Start a new order from the provider's profile or from the service you want.
3. Choose the service or services you need.
4. Enter a title and a description of the work.
5. Set the service date.
6. Confirm or adjust the area and your address details.
7. Choose a priority — normal or urgent.
8. Optionally attach a photo of the problem. You will be asked whether to **Take a Photo** or **Choose from Gallery**.
9. Submit the request.

The order is created with the status **Pending** and appears in your Orders tab. The provider is notified.

### Managing Orders

Open the **Orders** tab to see everything you have requested. Orders are grouped by status so you can see what is waiting, what is in progress, and what is finished.

Each order card shows the provider, the title, the date, the area, a progress indicator, and the order ID, which you can copy by tapping it.

Tap an order to open its details. The three-dot menu on an order card gives you the actions available for that order, which may include:

- Viewing or managing the order's photos
- Editing the order while it is still pending
- Cancelling the order
- Raising a complaint about the order

To add or change an order photo:

1. Open the order's photo options.
2. Choose to add, change, or add another photo.
3. Choose **Take a Photo** or **Choose from Gallery**.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.

### Chatting with a Professional

1. Open the **Messages** tab, or start a conversation from the provider's profile.
2. Select the conversation.
3. Type your message and send it.

See [Chat and Messaging](#7-chat-and-messaging) for attachments, voice messages, and translation.

### Sending Images

1. Open a conversation.
2. Tap the attachment button to open the **Share Content** sheet.
3. Choose **Gallery** to pick an existing image, or **Camera** to take a new one.
4. The image is uploaded and sent to the conversation.

### Camera and Gallery

Anywhere the app accepts a photo — chat attachments, your profile photo, order creation, and order photo management — you are offered both options. Choose **Take a Photo** to use the device camera, or **Choose from Gallery** to select an existing image. The first time you use the camera, Android will ask for permission.

### Notifications

Open the notifications screen from the Home tab. You will see order updates, new messages, review activity, and announcements from the administrators. Unread items are marked. Tap a notification to open the item it refers to, or use **Mark all read** to clear the unread state on everything at once.

### Reviews and Ratings

1. Open the profile of a provider you have worked with.
2. Tap **Add Review**.
3. Rate the provider on each criterion shown, using the stars.
4. Write a comment. A comment is required.
5. Submit the review.

If you have already reviewed that provider, submitting again updates your existing review rather than creating a second one. You can also edit a review you have already left. See [Reviews and Ratings](#9-reviews-and-ratings) for more detail.

### Profile Settings

Open the **Profile** tab to view and edit your account. You can change your name, email, phone, city, and address details, choose the languages you speak, select your favourite services, and set your preferred contact hours.

To change your profile photo, tap the camera button on your avatar and choose **Take a Photo**, **Choose Profile Photo**, or **Remove Profile Photo** if you already have one.

From the Profile tab you can also reach your complaints, your category requests, the Help Centre, and the logout option.

### Preferred Contact Hours

In the profile editor, find the **Preferred Contact Hours** section and tap the periods that suit you:

- **Morning**
- **Afternoon**
- **Evening**
- **Night**
- **Any Time**

You may select more than one specific period. **Any Time** means you have no restriction, so selecting it clears the individual periods, and selecting an individual period clears **Any Time**. Save your profile to store the change.

### AI Assistant / AI Features

The **AI Service Assistant** is available to customers from the Home tab, under the heading *Find the Right Provider with AI*. See [AI Features](#11-ai-features) for how to use it.

## 4. Professional Guide

The professional app has three tabs: **Home**, **Messages**, and **Profile**.

### Dashboard

The Home tab is your dashboard. It shows your incoming and active orders, counts for completed and cancelled work, your schedule, and an entry point to the AI Job Assistant.

### Receiving and Viewing Requests

New service requests arrive with the status **Pending** and appear on your dashboard. You are also notified. Tap an order to open its full details, which include the customer's information, the requested services, the service date, the area, the priority, and any photo the customer attached.

### Accepting Orders

From an order card's three-dot menu, or from the order details screen:

1. Open the pending order.
2. Tap **Accept**.
3. The order moves to **In Progress** and the customer is notified.

### Rejecting or Cancelling an Order

1. Open the order.
2. Tap **Reject** on a pending order, or **Cancel** on one already in progress.
3. Enter a cancellation reason. A reason is required.
4. Confirm.

The order moves to **Cancelled**, the reason is stored with it, and the customer is notified.

### Completing Work

1. Open the order once the work is done.
2. Tap the completion action.
3. The order moves to **Completed** and the customer is notified, which also allows them to review you.

### Chat

Open the **Messages** tab to see your conversations with customers, or start one from an order. You can send text, images, and voice messages, and translate a received message.

### Notifications

Your notifications screen lists order activity, new messages, and new reviews in a single list. Tap an item to open what it refers to, or use **Mark all read**.

### Profile

The **Profile** tab holds your public profile — the information customers see. You can edit your name and contact details, your work area and city, your experience and service description, and your profile photo.

### Services and Specialties

From your profile you can manage the services you offer, including their names, descriptions, and prices, and maintain your list of specialties. These determine which categories you appear under and what customers can request.

### Schedule and Availability

Your profile lets you set your working days and your working start and end times. These are shown on your public profile so customers know when you are available.

### Reviews

Your profile shows the reviews customers have left, with the overall rating and the breakdown by criterion. If a review is abusive or inaccurate, you can report it from the review, giving a reason. An administrator then reviews the report.

### AI Functionality

The **AI Job Assistant** is available from your dashboard. See [AI Features](#11-ai-features).

### Logout

Open the **Profile** tab and tap the logout button in the top-right of the header.

## 5. Contractor Guide

The contractor app has four tabs: **Home**, **Messages**, **Suppliers**, and **Profile**. The **Suppliers** tab is where you manage your workers and suppliers.

### Dashboard

The Home tab shows your orders with filters by status, your schedules, a search field, and entry points to the AI Crew & Order Planner.

### Orders

Orders appear on your dashboard grouped by status. Each card shows the customer, the title, the date, the area, the priority, and a progress indicator. Use the three-dot menu on a card for the actions available on that order.

### Order Details

Tap an order to open its details: the customer's information, the requested services, the date and area, any attached photo, and the workers currently assigned. The actions at the bottom change with the order's status.

### Accepting an Order and Assigning Workers

1. Open a pending order.
2. Tap **Accept & Assign Worker**.
3. Choose the workers you want from your roster.
4. Confirm the assignment.

The order moves to **In Progress** and the customer is notified.

While an order is in progress you can:

- **Manage Assigned Workers** — change who is on the job
- **Unassign All & Return to Pending** — clear the assignment and put the order back to pending
- **Cancel** — cancel the order, giving a reason
- Mark the order **Completed** when the work is done

### Workers and Suppliers

Open the **Suppliers** tab to manage your roster. For each worker you can see their name, specialties, and availability status.

To add a worker:

1. Open the **Suppliers** tab.
2. Start adding a new worker.
3. Enter their name and details.
4. Select their specialties.
5. Save.

You can open a worker to view their profile, edit their details, or remove them. Availability status values are **available**, **busy**, and **offline**.

### Messages and Chat

Open the **Messages** tab for your conversations with customers, or start one from an order. Text, images, voice messages, and on-demand translation all work the same as elsewhere in the app.

### Profile

The **Profile** tab holds your company profile: company name, contact details, work area and city, experience, service description, specialties, working days, and working hours. You can also change your profile photo here, and reach your complaints and the Help Centre.

### Schedules

Your dashboard includes a schedule view showing your active orders by date, colour-coded by status, so you can see what is coming up.

### AI Crew Planner

The **AI Crew & Order Planner** is available from your dashboard. See [AI Features](#11-ai-features).

### Logout

Open the **Profile** tab and use the logout option.

## 6. Admin Guide

The admin app has a Home dashboard, a Profile tab, and a set of management sections.

### Admin Dashboard

The Home tab summarises the platform: user counts, order counts by status, open complaints, and other statistics, with shortcuts into each management area.

### Users

Open **Users** to see every account. You can search and filter, open a user to see their full details, edit profile fields on their behalf, and apply account actions such as suspending or removing an account.

### Orders

Open **Orders** to see every order on the platform, grouped by status. From an order you can:

- View full details
- **Edit Order** — change the title, description, area, or service date
- **Approve** a pending order, moving it to In Progress
- **Complete** an order that is in progress
- **Cancel** an order
- **Reassign** the assigned worker on a contractor order

### Complaints Center

Open **Complaints Center** to triage complaints raised by customers, professionals, and contractors. You can open a complaint to see its details and the item it refers to, change its status, and act on it — including cancelling an order that a complaint is about.

### Review Management

Open **Review Management** to moderate reviews. You can hide a review, unhide it, soft-delete it, mark a reported review as safe, or edit its comment text. You can also configure the **review criteria** that customers rate against, which controls the star rows shown on the review form.

### Chat Management

Open **Chat Management** for the conversation tools. It has tabs for:

- **Conversations** — browse conversations, open a timeline, hide a message, and block or unblock a conversation
- **Requests** — handle "Request Admin Help" reports raised by users from their chat screens
- **Quick Replies** — maintain the shared library of canned messages

### Quick Replies

The Quick Replies tab lists every reply in the library. San3a ships with twelve general-purpose replies covering greetings, acknowledgements, asking for more information, asking for order details, location and preferred time, telling the user their request is being reviewed, asking them to wait, confirming resolution, offering more help, and closing politely.

From this tab you can:

- **Add Reply** — create a new quick reply
- **Edit** — change the text of an existing reply, including one of the defaults
- **Delete** — deactivate a reply so it no longer appears in chat; it can be reactivated later
- **Search** — filter the list by text

Quick replies are available to users in the chat screens, where they can be inserted into the message box.

### Broadcast Messages

To send an announcement:

1. Open **Send Broadcast Message** from the Admin Profile tab or the dashboard.
2. Choose the **Target Audience**. This is a multi-select — you can pick one group or several:
   - **All Users**
   - **Customers**
   - **Professionals**
   - **Contractors**
3. Selecting **All Users** means everyone, so it clears any individual groups you had selected. Selecting an individual group switches you out of **All Users**. Selecting all three individual groups is the same as everyone, so it collapses to **All Users**.
4. Enter the message text.
5. Tap **Send Now**.

If you try to send without selecting any audience, a validation message appears and nothing is sent. Everyone in the selected audiences receives the announcement as a notification, and nobody receives it twice. Recent broadcasts are listed on the Admin Profile tab with the audiences each was sent to.

### Categories

Open **Categories** to manage the service categories customers browse — adding, editing, and removing them — and to approve or reject the category requests customers submit.

### Notifications

Admins receive notifications for events that need attention, such as new complaints and category requests.

### Account and Profile

The **Profile** tab shows your admin account, platform statistics, the broadcast tools, and shortcuts to the management sections.

### Logout

Tap the logout button in the top-right of the Admin Profile header.

## 7. Chat and Messaging

Chat works the same way for customers, professionals, and contractors.

### Sending Messages

1. Open the **Messages** tab and select a conversation, or start one from a profile or an order.
2. Type in the message box at the bottom.
3. Send.

Messages show the time they were sent and whether they have been read. You can edit or delete a message you sent by long-pressing it. Unread counts appear on the conversation list.

### Image Attachments

1. Tap the attachment button to open the **Share Content** sheet.
2. Choose **Gallery** or **Camera**.
3. The image uploads and appears in the conversation.

### Camera and Gallery

**Gallery** opens your device's photo picker. **Camera** opens the device camera so you can take a new photo. If you deny the camera permission or back out without taking a photo, nothing is sent and a message explains what happened.

### Voice Messages

Tap the **Voice** option in the **Share Content** sheet to record and send a voice message. Recording requires microphone permission.

### Translation

Received text messages show a **Translate message** action. Tap it, then choose **Arabic**, **Hebrew**, or **English**. The translation appears beneath the original message, which is never replaced or altered. Tap **Hide translation** to collapse it. The same on-demand translation is available on provider descriptions, service details, reviews, and Help Centre articles.

### Quick Replies

Open the quick replies option in the chat screen to see the shared library of short messages. Tap one to insert it into your message box, where you can edit it before sending.

### Blocking and Reporting

You can block a conversation from the chat screen, and unblock it later. Blocked conversations cannot exchange new messages. You can also use **Request Admin Help** to raise a report about a conversation, which an administrator will see in Chat Management. Administrators may send a warning message into a conversation; it appears as a distinct card rather than a normal message bubble.

## 8. Orders and Statuses

An order has exactly four statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The customer has submitted the request. The provider has not accepted it yet. |
| **In Progress** | The provider has accepted the order and the work is underway. |
| **Completed** | The work has been finished. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled or rejected. A reason is stored with it. |

### Typical Lifecycle

1. The customer creates an order — it starts as **Pending**.
2. The provider accepts it — it becomes **In Progress**. A contractor assigns workers at this point.
3. The provider marks it finished — it becomes **Completed**.
4. The customer can then review the provider.

At any point before completion, the order may become **Cancelled** — by the provider with a reason, by the customer, or by an administrator.

### Actions Depend on Your Role

The actions offered on an order depend on who you are:

- **Customers** create orders, edit them while pending, manage order photos, cancel, and raise complaints.
- **Professionals** accept, complete, and reject or cancel with a reason.
- **Contractors** accept and assign workers, manage assigned workers, unassign all and return the order to pending, complete, and cancel with a reason.
- **Admins** approve, complete, cancel, edit order fields, and reassign a contractor's worker.

Completed and cancelled orders are final records — they are read-only and no further actions apply.

## 9. Reviews and Ratings

Reviews are written by customers about a provider they have worked with.

### Leaving a Review

1. Open the provider's profile.
2. Tap **Add Review**.
3. Rate each criterion using the stars. The criteria shown are configured by the administrators, so they may change over time.
4. Write a comment. This is required — you cannot submit an empty comment.
5. Submit.

Your overall rating is calculated from the criteria you rated. The provider is notified of the new review.

### Editing a Review

You can change a review you have already written. Reopen the review form for that provider, adjust the ratings and comment, and submit again. This updates your existing review rather than adding a new one — you have one review per provider, or one per order where the review is linked to a specific order.

### What Providers See

Providers see the reviews left about them and their overall rating on their profile. A provider cannot edit or delete a review, but can **report** one, giving a reason. Reported reviews go to the administrators.

### Moderation

Administrators can hide a review, restore it, remove it, mark a reported review as safe, or correct its comment text. Hidden and removed reviews no longer count towards a provider's public rating.

## 10. Notifications

Every role has a notifications screen inside the app. Notifications are delivered within San3a; the app does not send push notifications to your device's notification tray.

You may receive notifications for:

- Order activity — a new order, an order accepted, completed, rejected, or cancelled
- New reviews left about you
- Complaint activity
- Category request decisions
- Broadcast announcements from the administrators

Unread notifications are marked with an indicator, and the header shows how many are new.

- **Tap a notification** to open the item it refers to — for example, the related order or review.
- **Mark all read** clears the unread state on everything at once.

Read state is personal to your account.

## 11. AI Features

San3a includes four AI-backed features. All of them run on San3a's servers rather than on your device, and all of them require you to be signed in. Each feature is limited to a specific role, and there are daily usage limits with a short wait between requests — if you reach a limit, the app tells you.

AI output is a suggestion, not a guarantee. Always review it before acting on it.

### AI Service Assistant

- **Who can use it:** Customers
- **Where to find it:** The Home tab, under *Find the Right Provider with AI*
- **What you provide:** A description of your problem in your own words, in free text. You can write in your own language.

**What it does:** The assistant reads your description and suggests a service category from San3a's real catalogue, tells you whether a professional or a contractor is the better fit, drafts a title and an organised description for your request, suggests a priority, and explains why it chose that category. It may ask short follow-up questions to narrow things down.

**What you can do with the result:** Use the suggestion as the starting point for a service request, or browse the suggested category yourself. You are never obliged to accept it.

**How to use it:**

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Tap the AI Service Assistant entry.
3. Describe your problem in the **Describe your problem** field.
4. Tap **Analyze**.
5. Read the suggested category, provider type, title, and description.
6. Answer any follow-up questions shown, and proceed with a request if the suggestion looks right.

If the text is too short or too long, the app tells you before sending anything.

### AI Job Assistant

- **Who can use it:** Professionals
- **Where to find it:** The professional dashboard
- **What you provide:** One of your own orders

**What it does:** It prepares you for the job by producing a **Summary** of the work, questions worth asking the customer, a tools and materials list, suggested steps, safety warnings, and a draft message you can send to the customer.

**What you can do with the result:** Use it as a checklist before you travel, and copy the draft message into the conversation with the customer.

**How to use it:**

1. Open your dashboard.
2. Open the AI Job Assistant.
3. Select the order you want to prepare for.
4. Tap **Analyze**.
5. Review the sections produced.

### AI Crew & Order Planner

- **Who can use it:** Contractors
- **Where to find it:** The contractor dashboard
- **What you provide:** One of your own orders and a planning intent

**What it does:** It produces a staffing plan: a **Summary**, a **Recommended Crew Size**, **Crew Guidance**, questions for the customer, tools and materials, suggested steps, coordination notes, safety warnings, a draft customer message, and a ranked view of your own workers.

The worker ranking is based on your real roster data — whether a worker's specialty matches the job, how much work they already have, whether they are available, busy, or offline, and whether the job sits close to another commitment. The recommended crew size is advisory.

**What you can do with the result:** Use the ranking to decide who to assign, then assign those workers to the order in the normal way. The planner does not assign anyone for you.

**How to use it:**

1. Open your dashboard.
2. Open the **AI Crew & Order Planner**.
3. Select the order.
4. Choose a **Planning Intent**, such as **Plan Crew**.
5. Tap **Analyze**.
6. Review the plan and the ranked workers.

### Translation

- **Who can use it:** All signed-in users
- **Where to find it:** On individual chat messages, provider descriptions, service details, reviews, and Help Centre articles
- **What you provide:** Nothing — you just choose a target language

**What it does:** It translates that one piece of text into **Arabic**, **Hebrew**, or **English**, and shows the translation next to the original. The original is never changed or replaced, and translations are not saved.

**What you can do with the result:** Read content written in a language you do not speak. Tap **Hide translation** when you are finished.

## 12. Troubleshooting

**The camera does not open when I try to take a photo.**
Android asks for camera permission the first time you use it. If you denied it, open your device's Settings, find San3a under Apps, open Permissions, and allow Camera. Then try again. If your device has no camera available, use **Choose from Gallery** instead.

**I cannot select an image from the gallery.**
The app needs access to your photos. Check the same Permissions screen in your device settings. If you tap Cancel in the picker, nothing is attached — that is expected, and you can simply try again.

**Nothing loads, or an action fails with a connection message.**
San3a needs an internet connection for almost everything. Check that you are online, then retry. Screens that failed to load usually offer a retry.

**I cannot sign in.**
Check that your email address and password are correct and that you are connected to the internet. If you see a message saying your account is suspended or no longer available, contact an administrator — the account has been restricted rather than the password being wrong.

**A message or photo did not send.**
Confirm you are online and try again. If the conversation has been blocked, new messages cannot be sent and the app tells you so.

**The AI feature says I have reached a limit.**
AI features have a daily limit and a short cooldown between requests. Wait and try again later.

**My review will not submit.**
A comment is required, and every criterion must be rated. Fill in both and submit again.

**Text is in a language I do not read.**
Use the translation action on that message, description, review, or article, and pick Arabic, Hebrew, or English.

## 13. Privacy and Account Safety

**What other people can see.** Your name and profile photo are visible to the people you interact with. If you are a professional or a contractor, your public profile — services, specialties, work area, availability, and the reviews left about you — is visible to customers browsing the app.

**Keep your password to yourself.** Nobody from San3a will ask you for it. If you think someone else knows it, change it.

**Log out on shared devices.** If you use San3a on a device other people can access, log out when you are finished. Logging out clears the navigation history so the app cannot be reopened with the back button.

**Share only what is needed.** Photos you attach to an order or send in a conversation are visible to the other party and to administrators. Avoid sending documents or images containing personal identifiers, payment details, or anything you would not want reviewed.

**Reporting problems.** If another user behaves inappropriately, use the reporting tools: report a provider or a review from their profile, raise a complaint about an order, or use **Request Admin Help** from a conversation. Administrators can review conversations, hide messages, block conversations, moderate reviews, and restrict accounts.

**Administrator access.** Administrators can view and moderate platform content, including conversations, orders, and reviews, in order to handle complaints and keep the marketplace safe.

**Your contact preferences.** As a customer, use **Preferred Contact Hours** in your profile to tell providers when you are willing to be contacted.
